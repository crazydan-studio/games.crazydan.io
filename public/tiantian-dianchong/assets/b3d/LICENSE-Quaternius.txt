Assets used in this game:
- Ultimate Animated Animals (Fox, Shiba Inu) — models with skeletal animations
- Animated LowPoly Dinosaurs (T-Rex) — model with skeletal animations
- Farm Animals Animated (Pig) — model with skeletal animations
- Ultimate Nature Pack (trees, bushes, grass, rocks) — static models
- RPG Items Pack (bone toy, chicken leg) — static models
- Ultimate Home Interior (window) — static model
